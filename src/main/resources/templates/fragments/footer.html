DeepSpace Analytics

Deep-Space Communication Using Poisson and Geometric Models for Counting Transmission Drops and Identifying the First Successful Transmission.

Academic Project Scope

College Mathematics Project exploring spacecraft-to-Earth link reliability exclusively through two discrete probability distributions: the Poisson distribution for drop counts over fixed intervals and the Geometric distribution for first-success transmission attempts.
